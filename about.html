My Story

Elara Monroe

I picked up my first camera at seventeen — a battered film SLR my father left behind — and never looked back. Photography became my language; the way I made sense of the world's fleeting beauty. That early obsession led me through the halls of the New York Film Academy, two years assisting fashion photographers in Paris, and eventually to founding Lumière Studio in 2014.

Over twelve years I've photographed on six continents — from candlelit village weddings in Tuscany to couture runways in Milan, from intimate family portraits in Brooklyn lofts to corporate galas at the Lincoln Center. My images have appeared in Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, The New York Times and countless gallery exhibitions worldwide.

What drives me is simple: I want people to look at my photographs years from now and feel exactly what they felt in that moment. Not just remember it — but feel it again.

— Elara Monroe
